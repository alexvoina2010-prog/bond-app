# Directions (D1) — bond

Given: lilac, white and black (team). Alex's taste profile dislikes purple themes and gradients, so every card uses lilac as **one signal colour on white or paper and black ink**, never as a ground or a gradient. Lilac is never used for text on white (contrast); text is ink, and links use the deep lilac.

### 1. Caiet cu margine (Notebook margin)
- Feeling: familiar, warm, private.
- Why it fits: every student from grade 5 to 12 owns a notebook; a diary page is where people already write what they do not say out loud. The psychologist side stays a clean, quiet tool.
- Type: Instrument Serif (display) / Figtree (text), both with Romanian ș ț · scale 1.25.
- Colour: ground #FBFAF7 (paper), ink #161616, signal lilac #C8B6F2 (highlighter fill), deep lilac #5A3E9E (links, focus).
- Shape and density: radius 10, spacing base 4, airy.
- Signature: highlighter marks and one-line pen drawings in the margin (hands, a knot of two loops, a paper plane) · medium: drawn (Claude-authored SVG with one shared roughness filter).
- Precise layer: chat, lists and the dashboard are plain white with hairlines; drawings appear only on empty, success, onboarding and crisis screens.
- Risk: can look childish to 16–18-year-olds if the drawings get cute.
- References used for precision: claude (warm canvas plus quiet UI, colour roles), oura (wellbeing restraint).

### 2. Lampă de veghe (Night light)
- Feeling: calm, present, quiet.
- Why it fits: (inferred) students write when they are alone, often at night; a small light that says "someone is here" answers the fear of being alone with it.
- Type: Bricolage Grotesque (display) / DM Sans (text) · scale 1.2.
- Colour: light by day: ground #FFFFFF, ink #111111; dark by night: ground #0F0F10, ink #F4F4F5; signal lilac #B9A4F5, used only as the lamp.
- Shape and density: radius 16, spacing base 4, balanced.
- Signature: the lamp, a soft lilac dot that breathes; it shows presence (psychologist online), waiting and the breathing exercise on the crisis screen · medium: motion.
- Precise layer: everything except the lamp is monochrome.
- Risk: glows on black drift towards the purple "AI" look Alex dislikes; the lamp must stay one small element.
- References used for precision: oura.

### 3. Abțibild (Sticker sheet)
- Feeling: loud, peer-made, brave.
- Why it fits: (inferred) teenagers decorate laptops and phones with stickers; the peer chat should feel like it belongs to them, not to school.
- Type: Space Grotesk 700 (display) / DM Sans (text) · scale 1.333.
- Colour: ground #FFFFFF, ink #000000, signal lilac #CDBBFF flat fills with 2 px black outlines and hard offset shadows.
- Shape and density: radius 14, outline 2 px, spacing base 4, dense.
- Signature: the bond knot (two interlocked rounded loops) as logo, stickers and reaction stamps ("te cred", "nu ești singur") · medium: shape motif (SVG).
- Precise layer: the psychologist side drops the outlines and shadows.
- Risk: neo-brutalism is a recent web trend and can read flippant next to a crisis screen; the crisis screen drops all stickers.
- References used for precision: none.

## Independent ranking
Independent reviewer (saw only the brief and these cards): 1. Lampă de veghe, 2. Caiet cu margine, 3. Abțibild. Reasons: the lamp is the most memorable signature and keeps the psychologist side calm; the notebook is trustworthy but less distinctive and may look childish to 14–18; the stickers have the wrong tone for a frightened child and clash with a crisis screen. Biggest risk of its top pick: dark mode plus a glowing lilac dot drifts into the purple "AI" look; and the lamp is false presence if it shows "online" when nobody is.

## Recommendation
Lampă de veghe, with two guard rules: (1) light is the default and dark follows the device setting, so it is never dark-only; (2) the lamp is lit only when a real person is online; otherwise it is an unlit outline and the screen says when the psychologist answers. It also uses exactly the team's three colours. Rendered: `checks/directions-1280.png`. Copy note: headlines must be gender-neutral in Romanian ("singur" in card 2 is masculine).
