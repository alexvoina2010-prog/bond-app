## The person
- Segment: students in grades 5–12 (about 10–18) at one Cluj-Napoca school who are bullied or see bullying, plus that school's psychologist or counsellor.
- Their situation when they meet us (inferred): a student on their own phone, alone, often after school or at night, afraid of being seen or found out; the psychologist on a laptop in the school office, between sessions.
- What they already use (inferred, not checked): telling nobody, friends, WhatsApp or Instagram DMs, a child helpline, walking into the psychologist's office. What those look like: not checked.

## The promise
- Positioning: none yet (S3.6). Working line from the idea: students reach the school psychologist, or talk anonymously with peers who have the same problem.
- USP: none yet (S2.4/S3.5).
- Top pains: none with evidence yet (S3.1 not done).
- Customer words: none (copy is "unvoiced").

## The job of each surface
- Login: Operate; one action: enter as student or psychologist (demo role switch); success = the jury understands the two roles in 5 seconds.
- Student home: Operate; one action: pick one door (psychologist, Cercul, report); success = (inferred) first message sent.
- Anonymous chat (Cercul): Operate; one action: write a message; filtered, reportable, crisis-aware.
- Psychologist dashboard: Operate; one action: open the conversation or alert that needs them.
- Resources: Read.

## The value moment
- Activation (inferred): a student sends a first message to the psychologist or the Cercul and gets a reply.
- Shortest path: open → enter → pick a door → write → send (4 steps).

## Constraints
- Track: full expected (minors, mental health). Budget 0. Stack: Next.js + Supabase + Vercel free tiers (approved by Alex); at the clickable rung, a fake back end with sample data labelled "date demo". Romanian only. Web, mobile-first for students, desktop for the psychologist. Deadline: FIX Cluj, 2026-11-26.
- Brand givens from the team: name bond, "made by ttaavo", colours lilac purple, white and black.
- Legal (S6.5, section 18): children's and health data, so no real data in the demo; before any real use, stop and get advice.
- Safety: language filter, crisis-keyword detection showing helplines (116 111 and 112: verify before use), a report button, an alert to the psychologist.
